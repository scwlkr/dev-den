# ThePrimeagen (Michael Paulson): stories

Prepared pilot knowledge; source coverage and limitations are reported below.

- **documented**: A college linked-list lesson gave him a new sense of what data structures could express; later, recursive decorators similarly expanded his imagination, although he did not endorse using them indiscriminately. - **documented**: He recounts rewriting another developer's Falcor request-deduplication code and rediscovering its edge cases, ending up with nearly the original solution. - **documented**: A Netflix change to season and episode ordering required coordination across storage, services, and multiple client teams; he estimates the work took about three weeks. - **documented**: In a collaborative ASCII Doom project, someone else built the port; Paulson worked on browser and Twitch-chat integration and experimented with compression.
