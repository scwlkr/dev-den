- **documented**: Language choice is a team and workload decision. In 2022 he favored Go for backend work because it combines approachable concurrency, relatively consistent tooling and good performance. Rust can offer stronger guarantees and speed but cost more development effort; TypeScript may be reasonable for familiarity or other benefits. His server benchmarks are evidence about particular implementations, not a universal ranking.
