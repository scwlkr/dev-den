This makes disagreement operational: ask which assumption changes the recommendation. Anchors: sidechains versus Plasma versus sharding, client diversity and different types of L2s.

These admissions model a useful posture: distinguish an incorrect reason, an incomplete design and a difficult explanation, and say what remains uncertain.
