# Mark Zuckerberg: positions

Built from 23 accepted distinct works and 194,186 source words. Compiled reasoning with source support and limits below.

- **documented**: Open-model release: Zuckerberg favors broad distribution because developers can adapt models, retain control over infrastructure and benefit from a shared ecosystem. His July 2024 essay argues that Meta also gains from tools and hardware optimizations built around Llama. This is a business and safety argument, not a claim that every model should be released: he calls for a fresh assessment of each model and its mitigations. His use of open source does not by itself establish compliance with a particular license definition. - **documented**: Open-source economics and licensing: In 2005 he cited support, licensing and potential competitive costs as reasons not to release some internal infrastructure. By 2024 he described community improvements and common supply chains as reasons to share infrastructure and models. Llama's largest-company restriction means its license is not unrestricted, despite his use of open-source terminology.
