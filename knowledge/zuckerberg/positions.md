- **documented**: Open AI releases: In his July 2024 essay, Zuckerberg argues that open models give developers control, enable independent scrutiny and build an ecosystem of tools and optimized hardware. He does not promise to release every model: in July 2025 he cited both safety near superintelligence and the limited external usefulness of exceptionally large models as reasons for selectivity. Openness is his comparative judgment, not proof that every release is safer. Llama also has restrictions for the largest users; inspect its actual license rather than assuming unrestricted open source. - **documented**: AI release safety: His 2024 framework separates accidental harm from deliberate misuse. For the latter, he proposes red-teaming against what an attacker could already obtain from search, and distinguishes small actors from resource-rich states.
