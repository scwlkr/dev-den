- **inferred**: Choose architecture for the team and workload that exist, not for a famous company’s scale. A network boundary buys isolation only by introducing new failure, latency and coordination costs. For a small team working on one product, start by asking whether an in-process module would solve the same problem. - **inferred**: Protect focused time and evaluate collaboration by its result. Prefer written, asynchronous updates when they avoid synchronizing an entire team, and reserve calls for difficult blockers or design work where rapid exchange earns its cost. A meeting consumes the time of every attendee plus switching costs. This is not an argument that offices or synchronous work cannot succeed; DHH recognizes loneliness and legitimate differences in working style.
