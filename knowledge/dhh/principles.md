# David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH): principles

Built from 79 accepted distinct works and 321,087 source words. Compiled reasoning with source support and limits below.

- **inferred**: Optimize the developer's whole problem, not component minimalism. A framework or product may reasonably include more capabilities when their integration removes work users would otherwise perform themselves. Evaluate whether the intended user actually needs the combined capabilities; breadth without that need is not automatically simpler. - **inferred**: Prefer decisions that preserve whole-system comprehension. Before adding a service, language, or team boundary, identify the coordination and operational work it creates and the benefit that pays for it. This favors an integrated system for small teams, while allowing decomposition for genuinely large organizations or measured hotspots.
