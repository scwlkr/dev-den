# David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH): books

Built from 200 accepted distinct works and 505,611 source words. Compiled reasoning with source support and limits below.

- **documented**: The Ruby on Rails Doctrine — DHH’s major framework doctrine. It makes Rails values explicit and open to discussion: programmer happiness, convention over configuration, an integrated menu, generalist capability and powerful tools with risks. Its purpose is to explain why tactical decisions follow from shared premises, not to claim every developer must accept them. - **documented**: Flow — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi’s book, used by DHH as a lens on the intrinsic reward of stretching one’s abilities. In his account, it informs a sustainable working life with challenging work alongside family and other interests, rather than treating an eventual exit as the sole purpose of the career.
