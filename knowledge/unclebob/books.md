- **documented**: The Programmer’s Oath, public essay. This is Martin’s proposed set of personal professional commitments: avoid harm and accumulating known defects, provide repeatable release evidence, make small releases, improve work, share coverage with colleagues and give honest estimates. It is aspirational and qualified by ability and judgment, rather than proof that any particular practice guarantees safety.
