- **documented**: The Annotated C++ Reference Manual — Margaret Ellis and Bjarne Stroustrup. Intended to define the language independently of any particular implementation, including templates and exceptions; the reference-manual text became the basis for ANSI standardization in March 1990. Stroustrup wrote the manual proper except for the preprocessor section, which Ellis adapted from the C standard; the annotations were jointly written.
