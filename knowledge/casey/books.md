- **documented**: Performance-Aware Programming Series — Muratori’s educational series, established here by its public 2023 announcement. Its thesis is that ordinary programmers can avoid large performance mistakes by understanding generated code, CPU behavior, and memory, without all becoming specialist optimizers. The announced method combines assembly reading, workload measurement, hardware estimates, and comparison of implementations. The supplied announcement describes a teaching plan, not results from the paid course. - **documented**: Stable Filtering — an authored essay series established by the supplied Part 2.
