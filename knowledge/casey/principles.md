- **inferred**: Make performance trade-offs consciously. Estimate or measure the cost and future repair burden before accepting a slower design; ignorance of the cost is not the same as a deliberate trade. The fastest option need not win when the product has other constraints. Corroboration: high (4 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Do not turn a conditional observation into an excuse to skip investigation. Some products have concentrated hotspots, some optimizations do not pay financially, and some performance is already sufficient. None of those possibilities establishes the answer for a particular product without checking. Counterexamples rebut a blanket rule; they do not prove its opposite is universal.
