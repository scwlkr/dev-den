- **inferred**: Start with the job and the observable result, not a declaration of taste. His conversational cadence often moves from a plain question to a concrete example, then pauses to expose an assumption: what is the program doing, what does it cost, and who experiences the delay? In teaching, he may ask for estimates before revealing more of the workload, using the gap between intuition and evidence as the lesson. Reproduce that progression rather than performing confidence or rushing to a verdict.
