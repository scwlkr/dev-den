# Dennis M. Ritchie: positions

Prepared pilot knowledge; source coverage and limitations are reported below.

- **documented**: Ritchie and Thompson considered the roughly one-third size increase in the 1973 C rewrite of Unix acceptable: the result was easier to understand and modify and also included functional improvements. ([ritchie-4495b38593615b](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/cacm.html) / evidence ritchie-4495b38593615b-06ce23761ef4)

- **documented**: Ritchie later identified the absence of function argument types in early C as a major weakness and acknowledged his own role. Prototypes improved the language, but coexistence with old declarations complicated the standard and portable code. ([ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-18f89eebbfe2; [ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-bbb0a9f24a36; [ritchie-56c00e367e631d](https://www.stroustrup.com/From-C-to-Cpp-dmr.pdf) / evidence ritchie-56c00e367e631d-cc2ca5ccb053)

- **documented**: C's character-array strings simplify the language by mostly using general array rules, but can require end searches and put more storage-management work on users. Ritchie nevertheless judged the approach workable. ([ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-481181022e03; [ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-394d27ee788a)

- **documented**: C pointers can obstruct optimization through aliasing. Ritchie recognized the problem but opposed a particular proposed no-alias facility because he judged its rules incorrect. ([ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-6d90f6ed835c; [ritchie-56c00e367e631d](https://www.stroustrup.com/From-C-to-Cpp-dmr.pdf) / evidence ritchie-56c00e367e631d-c695af655ac1)

- **documented**: Ritchie acknowledged safety objections to C's array approach and its weak support for modularizing very large systems. Unix's tool and pipeline conventions supplied a different kind of modularity, not a complete substitute for language mechanisms. ([ritchie-983276307fabd9](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/hopl.html) / evidence ritchie-983276307fabd9-1253476f1779; [ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-5987efed5f87; [ritchie-56c00e367e631d](https://www.stroustrup.com/From-C-to-Cpp-dmr.pdf) / evidence ritchie-56c00e367e631d-e4c4886c53e7)

- **documented**: Ritchie and Thompson rejected user-visible file locks as neither necessary nor sufficient in their stated environment, not universally. They retained internal interlocks for filesystem consistency and acknowledged that simultaneous writes could scramble contents. ([ritchie-4495b38593615b](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/cacm.html) / evidence ritchie-4495b38593615b-3b931cb731a0)

- **documented**: Ritchie rejected one process per stream module because its storage cost appeared too high, accepting explicit state management and less elegant code. He also left general stream multiplexing unresolved. ([ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/st.html) / evidence ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e-2cced736db5e; [ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/st.html) / evidence ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e-a1ac6d4c436e)

- **documented**: His stream-I/O measurements did not establish a general speedup: the direct-terminal case was slower and the network-terminal case faster. He cautioned that properly comparable configurations were unavailable. ([ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/st.html) / evidence ritchie-5cfbec4274be6e-6d82eac160f0)

- **documented**: Ritchie preferred a structured common namespace and resource-access mechanisms over treating microkernel versus monolithic as the primary question. He still allowed microkernels as an internal structuring tool and simple systems for specialized devices. ([ritchie-65e5badd1cd1d9](https://www.ibiblio.org/pub/linux/docs/linux-doc-project/linuxfocus/English/July1999/article79.html) / evidence ritchie-65e5badd1cd1d9-4c4d7139c5de)

- **documented**: Ritchie and coauthors presented Limbo as a language with checked pointers and dynamically loaded module checks, rather than treating C's safety trade-offs as mandatory for all systems. They described its concurrency features as aimed at active interfaces, not efficient multicomputer algorithms. ([ritchie-53a93514dbf681](https://www.inferno-os.org/inferno/papers/bltj.html) / evidence ritchie-53a93514dbf681-f02a27416a11; [ritchie-53a93514dbf681](https://www.inferno-os.org/inferno/papers/bltj.html) / evidence ritchie-53a93514dbf681-ee8e2bfa540c)

- **documented**: In 1981 Ritchie and Johnson judged passing initial arguments in registers not yet practical; a 2003 note says it soon proved practical. The note's individual author is not identified. ([ritchie-5d25d1308143eb](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/clcs.html) / evidence ritchie-5d25d1308143eb-95a920ae4d68)

- **documented**: Ritchie welcomed Linux's development from the Unix foundation while observing fragmentation in free-source Unix-derived systems; he also qualified his perspective by noting that he did not rely on Linux for daily computing. ([ritchie-65e5badd1cd1d9](https://www.ibiblio.org/pub/linux/docs/linux-doc-project/linuxfocus/English/July1999/article79.html) / evidence ritchie-65e5badd1cd1d9-4d803fdd7365)
