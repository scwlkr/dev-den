# Dennis M. Ritchie: principles

Built from 30 accepted distinct works and 129,100 source words. Compiled reasoning with source support and limits below.

- **inferred**: Keep a design compact by choosing what it will not do, but verify that the omitted cases are tolerable for the intended users. Unix’s narrowly targeted communication mechanisms worked for their original environment; Ritchie also recorded weaknesses from missing synchronization and more general I/O.  Application: enumerate exclusions and their workarounds before using compactness as the deciding benefit. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Treat portability as a property to engineer and test, not as an automatic reward for writing C. Audit pointer assumptions, runtime conventions, operating-system interfaces and the cost of the target implementation. Ritchie’s 1993 history says portability was not C’s original prime goal; the 1978 portability work describes concrete steps and remaining target expertise. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). ([ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-cdac9257111c; [ritchie-be88b7b72521cb](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/portpap.html) / evidence ritchie-be88b7b72521cb-ca6d166a6742; [ritchie-c6c3e1227aedef](https://ftpmirror2.infania.net/sites/unixarchive/Documentation/OralHistory/transcripts/ritchie.htm) / evidence ritchie-c6c3e1227aedef-276233fe0db0; 3 independent supporting works)

- **inferred**: Judge an abstraction by what it lets independently built components share. A common byte stream, file/device interface or resource namespace can remove repeated special cases. Qualification: uniformity is a design effort, not proof that every device has identical semantics, framing or performance. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Make examples and measurements carry their qualifications. Distinguish a workload-specific observation from a general result, and a numerical estimate from a comprehensive study. The block-size experiment, C cost estimates and stream timings each came with explicit caveats.  Application: report the tested configuration and the strongest counterexample alongside a proposed optimization. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Prefer a demonstrated need and a good fit over an isolated feature. First try existing primitives; add a new one when serious, demonstrable inefficiency or a genuinely unsupported use warrants the interface cost. This does not mean that missing synchronization or asynchronous I/O was harmless: Ritchie named them as weaknesses. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). ([ritchie-504320fb4a7e46](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/retro.html) / evidence ritchie-504320fb4a7e46-efadf4f35931; [ritchie-504320fb4a7e46](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/retro.html) / evidence ritchie-504320fb4a7e46-8a9c03798f09; [ritchie-7ca8eec9ac8af9](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/hist.html) / evidence ritchie-7ca8eec9ac8af9-9ec9abf737af; 2 independent supporting works)

- **inferred**: Check practical safety, not just the elegance of a permission model. Audit default modes, privileged programs, authentication, raw-device access and resource exhaustion. Ritchie’s Unix retrospective distinguishes theoretical adequacy from practical failure; the Styx and Inferno papers describe connection and namespace controls as parts of their respective designs, not as automatic guarantees for every deployment. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Preserve compiler freedom deliberately, but make the programmer-facing limit explicit. Historical C left ordinary expression order unspecified to allow efficient evaluation; different calling arrangements made a fixed argument order costly or complicated. Application: separate side effects that depend on order rather than relying on one implementation. The historical manual is not a substitute for the current language standard. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). ([ritchie-50a70b87adf27f](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/cman.pdf) / evidence ritchie-50a70b87adf27f-24a1124aa374; [ritchie-5d25d1308143eb](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/clcs.html) / evidence ritchie-5d25d1308143eb-d675acef5093; 2 independent supporting works)

- **inferred**: Use types to expose obligations, but inspect what a type qualifier actually guarantees. Ritchie supported function prototypes because they improve checking and conversion, while warning that a pointer to const did not establish that the object could not change through another route. Application: do not derive an optimizer or concurrency guarantee from a qualifier without checking its exact semantics. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). ([ritchie-56c00e367e631d](https://www.stroustrup.com/From-C-to-Cpp-dmr.pdf) / evidence ritchie-56c00e367e631d-c0fb38435c90; [ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html) / evidence ritchie-91fa14fdc827e6-bbb0a9f24a36; 2 independent supporting works)

- **inferred**: Acknowledge the cost of powerful low-level operations. C’s pointers and arrays support systems work and flexible data access, but aliasing complicates optimization and its array approach raises legitimate safety concerns. Application: choose the language and checks for the workload rather than claiming C is either universally unsafe or inherently adequate for every environment. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Keep language design connected to implementation and the surrounding environment. A formally attractive feature can reveal unexpected consequences when implemented; a usable language also needs available compilers and interaction with real systems. Qualification: Ritchie did not present these factors as a complete explanation of C’s adoption. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Design for change in time as well as movement between machines. Encapsulate a translation whose data format may evolve, and keep a system modifiable when unchanged portability cannot exploit a substantially different machine. Application: identify both platform-dependent code and assumptions about external databases. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Use the system and leave room to revise it. Thompson and Ritchie attributed part of Unix’s development to designers encountering its deficiencies directly and having modifiable source available. Ritchie’s retrospective also examines defects rather than treating the installed design as final.  This supports feedback and revision; it does not establish that designer use replaces independent testing. Corroboration: medium (2 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). ([ritchie-4495b38593615b](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/cacm.html) / evidence ritchie-4495b38593615b-6f6b67dafcaa; [ritchie-504320fb4a7e46](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/retro.html) / evidence ritchie-504320fb4a7e46-aa6b408f4508; 2 independent supporting works)

- **inferred**: Do not make a general mechanism carry obligations its real use does not require without examining the alternatives. Early Unix replaced a general message facility with wait because the latter addressed the observed shell synchronization need more directly; the old use also had a bug. Qualification: Ritchie said a genuine need for general messages could have justified repairing it. Corroboration: low (1 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Make uncertainty and correction part of the design record. Ritchie admitted uncertainty about declarator syntax and about a general stream-multiplexing mechanism; Johnson and Ritchie’s calling-sequence report also preserves a later correction about register arguments. Application: record open questions and later counterevidence, rather than rewriting tentative judgments as permanent conclusions. Corroboration: high (3 distinct works; this measures source count, not truth). - **inferred**: Credit the actual originator and distinguish a retrospective explanation from established causation. - **inferred**: Treat simplicity as a trade, not a claim that nothing is lost. Identify the version actually available to the original developers, distinguish a published notation from what a compiler accepted, and disclose transcription changes. Ritchie’s BCPL archive commentary follows these practices; the reproduced manual itself is Martin Richards’s work, not Ritchie’s.
