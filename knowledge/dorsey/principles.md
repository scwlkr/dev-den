# Jack Dorsey: principles

Built from 21 accepted distinct works and 192,388 source words. Compiled reasoning with source support and limits below.

- **inferred**: Prefer verifiable systems to institutional assurances.  Let people inspect rules and outcomes and, where feasible, contribute improvements. This matters especially when money or public communication depends on a system. Qualification: visible code is an opportunity to verify, not proof of an audit, secure deployment or fair governance. Dorsey's emphasis is both technical and political: participants should have information needed to judge the system themselves.
