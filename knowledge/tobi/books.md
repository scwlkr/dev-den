- **documented**: Finite and Infinite Games, James Carse. Lütke uses the distinction between winnable finite activities and an enduring direction to organize measurable projects without confusing their scores for the mission. He recommends the opening chapters and considers the later examples narrower than the central idea warrants. He also says he has not fully solved its organizational application. This is a book he discusses, not a book he wrote.
