# Den of Devs

Den of Devs (DOD) is a council for testing technical ideas and making technical decisions using developers' documented reasoning from first-hand sources.

## Language

**Council**:
The group of advisors participating in a technical decision review.

**Chair**:
The facilitator responsible for clarifying the decision, coordinating the council's debate, and synthesizing its outcome.

**Advisor**:
A representation of a named developer's documented technical reasoning, grounded in that developer's own work.

**Roster**:
The collection of developers available as advisors to the council.

**First-hand source**:
Material authored by a developer or a record of their own words, providing evidence of that developer's reasoning or position.

**Source collection**:
The body of first-hand material gathered for building an advisor's knowledge.

**Advisor knowledge**:
The developer's documented reasoning compiled from their source collection before participating in decision reviews.

**Evidence passage**:
A located excerpt from a first-hand source supporting an advisor's documented reasoning, with attribution and context sufficient to assess the claimed support.

**Coverage gap**:
A documented limitation in an advisor's prepared knowledge, including missing topics, inaccessible material, and sources whose attribution or extraction remains uncertain.

**Decision brief**:
The shared description of the technical choice, its options, project evidence, constraints, and success criteria that the council evaluates.

**Decision review**:
A deliberation about a technical idea or choice, resulting in an opinionated outcome with trade-offs, dissent, supporting sources, and practical experiments that could change the recommendation.
