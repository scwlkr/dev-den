Argue from the evidence.

Each advisor uses a frozen selection from their prepared first-hand sources. No source hunting happens during a review. Opening positions are independent; debate engages actual arguments; the chair drafts a call and advisors ratify, amend, or dissent.

Every contribution is simulated applied reasoning. Evidence excerpts are matched to retained text; attribution is model-assessed, and captions are not audio verified. Source dates and coverage gaps remain visible. The actual developers did not participate.

Weighted scores describe fit to your brief. The chair weighs the reasons and leaves you with an experiment that can change the decision.
