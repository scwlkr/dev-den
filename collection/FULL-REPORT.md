Attribution and semantic support are model-assessed. Course chapters are one work. Video-caption text is not audio-verified; missing visual/code context remains a limitation.

The original pilot ledger is reused, including all prior charges and any unresolved reservations. The current allocation reflects the owner’s latest authorization to use existing credits. Costs are token/tool estimates, not invoices. Raw source text, metadata, model outputs and passage evidence remain local and Git-ignored.

Principle corroboration is computed from cited distinct work identities: high for three or more, medium for two, low for one. It measures repeat support, not factual truth. The depth and structure comparisons come from [roast-room’s workflow](https://github.com/nateherkai/roast-room/blob/main/.claude/skills/newroaster/SKILL.md) and [brain specification](https://github.com/nateherkai/roast-room/blob/main/.claude/skills/newroaster/references/brain-spec.md). The installed summarize tool was evaluated in five extraction-only cases and not adopted; see [the evaluation](README.md#summarize-evaluation).

New retrieval rejects translated English caption tracks and retains the selected underlying language. Earlier caption snapshots did not retain track-level language metadata; their original-language provenance cannot be certified retrospectively from the local records. No claim of audio verification is made.
