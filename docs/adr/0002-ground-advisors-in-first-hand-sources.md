# Ground advisors in first-hand sources

Den of Devs follows roast-room's reasoning model: advisors apply principles compiled from their own first-hand sources to the current decision and cite the principles they use. Generated advice may reason beyond an existing quotation, but must not fabricate quotations or invent historical positions; this permits an opinionated debate about new problems without treating simulated advice as the actual developer's stated conclusion.

Source dates and changes of view are handled during knowledge compilation, following roast-room's approach, without a separate chronology interface.
