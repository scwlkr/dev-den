# Retain auditable sources and automate the pilot

Retain extracted source text, source details, passage-level evidence, and compiled advisor knowledge so quotations can be checked and knowledge rebuilt without fetching every original again. Follow roast-room's knowledge structure; remove bulky downloads and intermediates after verified extraction, and keep bulk source text outside Git.

The pilot uses available transcripts and captions; recordings requiring new transcription remain reported gaps. It runs discovery, retrieval, extraction, compilation, and verification without per-source approvals, holding out ambiguous or unusable sources while continuing with usable material. DHH, Dennis Ritchie, and The Primeagen are the initial scope, with a $25 total spending ceiling covering model calls and paid tools, followed by a cost-and-coverage report before further spending or expansion.
